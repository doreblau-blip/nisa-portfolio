Turkcell Akademi
Geleceği Yazanlar

For Turkcell's Geleceği Yazanlar, I developed a distinctive visual language that brings together coding culture, digital interfaces and contemporary graphic design. Electric blue and yellow, UI elements, typography and code references are combined with AI-assisted imagery to create a dynamic and recognizable system. I transformed educational communication from conventional corporate content into a more energetic, contemporary and campaign-driven visual experience.
